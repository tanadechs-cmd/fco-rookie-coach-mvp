# Domain glossary

## Educational Hub answer

An answer supported by an approved FC Online Educational Hub knowledge record. The answer may include the original learning-content link.

## AI fallback

An answer generated only after the product knowledge lookup finds no matching Educational Hub answer. It may answer low-risk, stable gameplay or general FC Online questions, but must not guess about accounts, login, payments, bans, missing items, rewards, live events, patches, prices, or player-specific account data.

## Escalation

Routing a question that should not be answered by the AI fallback to an appropriate next channel: customer support for account cases, the Educational Hub for browsing approved learning content, the FC Online Discord community for peer help, or the unanswered-question queue for future knowledge coverage.

## Response action

A structured next step returned with an answer so the product can render a trusted button instead of relying on a URL embedded in conversational text. Supported destinations are the Educational Hub, FC Online Discord, customer support, and the unanswered-question queue.

## Unanswered question

A question the user explicitly chooses to send to the team after the product cannot provide a satisfactory answer. Recording it is an intentional user action, not an automatic consequence of low confidence. The MVP record excludes Garena UID and the full conversation history.
